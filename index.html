I'm Rishu, a UX designer
with an eye for the essential.

My work is guided by a quiet philosophy — that the finest experiences are never accidental. They emerge from deep listening, careful observation, and an unflinching commitment to the people who will inhabit the spaces I design.

I am drawn to clients who appreciate the timeless over the trendy, the considered over the conspicuous. I believe great design need not announce itself — it simply works, beautifully and without effort.

Currently pursuing CSIT, I bridge the world of technology and aesthetics, translating complex human needs into experiences that feel, above all, natural.

My Approach

How I work.

I

Discover

I listen before I draw. Understanding the people, the context, and the constraints is where every worthy solution begins.

II

Define

Clarity before creativity. I distil research into a sharp design brief — the north star that keeps every decision purposeful.

III

Design

From rough sketches to refined interfaces. I explore broadly, then narrow with rigour until the solution feels inevitable.

IV

Deliver

Handoff that empowers developers. Detailed specifications, design tokens, and continued collaboration until launch.
